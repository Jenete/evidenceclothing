About Evidence Clothing

Evidence Clothing is a streetwear brand rooted in Khayelitsha – built on hustle, confidence and creativity. Each piece is designed to carry a story, from everyday essentials to statement items that turn heads.

We focus on small runs and quality fits, so when you rock Evidence you’re not just wearing clothes – you’re wearing proof of where you’re from, where you’re going, and what you stand for.
